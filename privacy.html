Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you sign in to and use the Elimination Leaderboard, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Use

  • Account & Sign-In Data: When you sign in using Google or Discord, the service receives basic profile information including your display name, email address (if provided), avatar image URL, and account identifier (Firebase UID or Discord User ID).
  • Game & Match Telemetry: Linked in-game usernames, elimination counts, match outcomes, and active character selections are synced in real time via Overwolf and stored in Firebase Realtime Database to display live standings and track player challenges.

Storage and Retention

Leaderboard standings, user account links, and challenge records are stored securely in Google Firebase Realtime Database. Authentication tokens and session identifiers may be stored locally in your browser so you remain signed in across visits. You can sign out at any time using the account menu.
