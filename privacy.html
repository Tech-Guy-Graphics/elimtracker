Privacy Policy

Effective October 5, 2026

This policy explains what information is used when you sign in to and use the Elimination Leaderboard, why it is used, and the choices available to you.

Information We Use

When you choose Google sign-in, the service receives basic account information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, profile photo, and account identifier (Firebase UID). The UID is used to check whether the site operator has approved access for your account.

The operator maintains approved UIDs and associated game usernames in a private Google spreadsheet. The leaderboard itself may display game usernames, player names, profile image URLs, elimination totals, characters, participation status, and placement from its public spreadsheet.

How Information Is Used

  • To authenticate your Google account and verify leaderboard access.
  • To associate your game username with your account and mark your matching leaderboard entry.
  • To display leaderboard information and provide search, filtering, and refresh features.
  • To maintain and troubleshoot the sign-in and leaderboard service.

Google Services

Sign-in and account verification are provided by Google and Firebase Authentication. The site sends a Firebase sign-in token to its Google Apps Script access service for verification. The token is used to validate the account and is not intentionally stored by the site operator.

The site uses the Google Sheets API to retrieve leaderboard data. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. The service does not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Storage and Retention

Firebase Authentication may keep sign-in session information in your browser so you remain signed in. You can sign out using the account menu. The operator-managed UID and game-username mapping remains in the private access spreadsheet until the operator changes or removes it. Leaderboard information remains subject to the spreadsheet owner's updates and deletion.

Your Choices

You can stop using the service and sign out at any time. To request correction or removal of an account-to-game-name mapping, contact Tech Guy Graphics through the contact channel provided by the site operator. Information in the public leaderboard spreadsheet may be visible to anyone who can access that spreadsheet.

You can also manage your Google account's connections to third-party services in your Google Account settings.

Changes and Contact

This policy may be updated when the service or its data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when updates are made. For privacy questions or requests, contact Tech Guy Graphics through the contact channel provided by the site operator.
